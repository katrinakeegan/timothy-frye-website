Books

Workplace Politics: How Politicians and Employers Subvert Elections

Oxford University Press, 2025
With Ora John Reuter and David Szakonyi

Drawing on surveys of managers and employees in eight countries and a close look at Russia, this book examines the subtle and not so subtle ways that employers mobilize their workers to vote, and when politicians are able to enlist them to do so.

Weak Strongman: The Limits of Power in Putin's Russia

Princeton University Press, 2021

Rather than treating Russian politics as an extension of Vladimir Putin's worldview or of Russia's unique history, Weak Strongman emphasizes Russia's similarities to other autocracies and highlights the difficult trade-offs that confront the Kremlin on issues from election fraud and repression to propaganda and foreign policy.

  • Shortlisted, 2022 Pushkin House Book Prize
  • 2022 Toronto International Festival of Authors

Property Rights and Property Wrongs: How Power, Institutions, and Norms Shape Economic Conflict in Russia

Cambridge University Press, 2017

Using surveys and survey experiments with Russian firms from 2000 to 2012, this book shows how political power, institutions, and social norms shape the security of property rights in everyday business disputes.

Building States and Markets After Communism: The Perils of Polarized Democracy

Cambridge University Press, 2010

A study of state-building and market-building in 25 post-communist countries from 1990 to 2004, showing that democracy supports economic reform and stronger state institutions when political polarization is low, and that polarization undercuts those gains.

  • Best Book Award, Comparative Democratization Section, American Political Science Association
  • 2011 Hewett Prize, Association for Slavic, East European, and Eurasian Studies

The Policy World Meets Academia: Designing U.S. Policy toward Russia

American Academy of Arts and Sciences, 2010
Co-edited with Timothy Colton and Robert Legvold

Brokers and Bureaucrats: Building Market Institutions in Russia

University of Michigan Press, 2000

  • 2001 Hewett Prize, American Association for the Advancement of Slavic Studies
